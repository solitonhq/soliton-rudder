# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the spirit and principles of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
(© the Contributor Covenant authors, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). A short summary
follows; the linked text is authoritative.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this community to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, respectful and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this
Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report problems privately to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), by contacting a maintainer through
their GitHub profile or through the [contact page](https://solitonhq.github.io/contact/). A dedicated conduct address will
be added there once the project mailbox exists. Please do not use the security advisory form for conduct reports; it is for
vulnerabilities. Reports are handled confidentially, and the people involved are told what was decided. A maintainer who is
the subject of a report, or is close to the people involved, steps aside from handling it.
